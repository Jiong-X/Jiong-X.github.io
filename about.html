About me

I'm an MSc student in Cognitive and Decision Sciences at University College London, currently working on my thesis at the MetaLab under the supervision of Professor Stephen Fleming, Dr Jason da Silva Castanheira, and Dr Kevin O'Neil. Before UCL I completed an MA in Cognitive Science at the University of Edinburgh , where I did my honours dissertation with Dr Neil R Bramley.

I have a broad interest in cognition. To answer questions about how the mind functions, I think it is crucial to utilise knowledge from various disciplines, including psychology, neuroscience, and computer science. I'm drawn to questions about how people process information in ways that make sense of the world around them — how we build internal models of complex environments, and what shortcuts we use to act effectively without getting overwhelmed.
